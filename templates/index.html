About the project

Making borehole maintenance more proactive

Rural communities depend on functioning water points for reliable access to clean water. When a borehole fails, identifying the problem and organizing maintenance can take time.

This project uses borehole survey data and machine learning to estimate the risk of failure. The aim is to help responsible teams identify which water points may need attention first.

The system also supports role-based access for Trainees, Experts, and District Officers.

Project goal: support better maintenance decisions through data, risk assessment, and coordinated work.

How it works

From borehole data to informed action

The intended process connects water-point information with risk estimates and maintenance planning.

1

Collect Data

Use available borehole survey and water-point information as input to the system.

2

Estimate Failure Risk

Apply a trained machine-learning model to estimate the risk associated with each eligible record.

3

Plan Maintenance

Use the risk estimates to help responsible teams prioritize inspections and maintenance activities.

Who uses the system?

One system. Three user roles.
